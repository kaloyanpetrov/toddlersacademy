2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

When you submit an enrollment inquiry through our website, we collect the following data about the child and parent / legal guardian:

  • Child's full name, date of birth, and age at entry
  • Preferred kindergarten location (Q-Center or The Bells)
  • Parent / guardian full name
  • Parent / guardian contact email address and phone number
  • Any additional information you voluntarily include in the inquiry

When you contact us directly by email or phone, we retain the content of that communication and your contact details.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical data is collected automatically, subject to your cookie consent:

  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation path
  • IP address (anonymised where applicable)
  • Cookie identifiers and consent preferences
  • Referral source (the URL that led you to our site)

4. Processing of Children's Data

As a kindergarten, we necessarily process personal data about children. We observe the following safeguards:

  • We only collect children's data through their parents or legal guardians. We do not collect data directly from children.
  • The minimum data required for an enrollment inquiry is collected; we do not ask for more information than is necessary (data minimisation principle).
  • All children's data is stored securely and accessed only by authorised staff.
  • If you believe we have received information about your child incorrectly or without authorisation, please contact us immediately at toddlersacademykindergarten@gmail.com and we will promptly review and delete that data.

5. Data Retention

Category Retention Period

Email and phone communication records Up to 3 years from last contact

At the end of each retention period, data is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the GDPR and the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right What it means
Access (Art. 15) Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how it is used.
Rectification (Art. 16) Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
Erasure (Art. 17) Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten") where there is no overriding legal basis for retention.
Restriction of processing (Art. 18) Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances (e.g., while accuracy is disputed).
Data portability (Art. 20) Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transfer it to another controller, where technically feasible.
Objection (Art. 21) Object to processing based on our legitimate interest. We will stop processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.
Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) Withdraw previously given consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at toddlersacademykindergarten@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days in line with Art. 12 GDPR. Requests are free of charge unless manifestly unfounded or excessive.

8. Cookie Policy

8.1 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by the websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to website owners. Cookies set by the website operator are called "first-party cookies"; cookies set by other parties are called "third-party cookies".

8.2 Types of cookies we use

Category Description Can be disabled?
Necessary Essential for the website to function correctly. These include cookies that record your cookie consent decision. The website cannot function properly without them. No
Functional Enable enhanced functionality and personalisation, such as remembering language preferences or previously viewed content. Yes
Statistics / Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting and reporting information anonymously (e.g., pages visited, session duration). Yes
Marketing Used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other websites. We currently use social media links (Facebook, Instagram) which may set third-party cookies. Yes

For the full, up-to-date list of specific cookies used on this website, see our Cookie Declaration.

8.3 Managing your cookie preferences

You can review and update your cookie preferences at any time:

  • Open cookie settings (footer of any page on this website)
  • Through your browser settings (see guidance below)

Be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

8.4 Browser cookie controls

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our website
  • Restricted access to personal data (need-to-know basis)
  • Regular review of data handling practices

Despite these measures, no transmission over the internet is completely secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

  • Adequacy decisions by the European Commission where applicable
